# Firm benchmarks: clickable prototype

A design prototype for the law-firm side of client.legal.io: market rates, salaries and matter costs (Benchmark), plus a Visibility page (Win Work).

- One file, no build: `index.html`.
- All numbers are illustrative. Harlow & Pierce and every peer firm shown are fictional.
- Links keep the view, for example `#rates?cmp=estimate` or `#matters?cmp=plan`.
